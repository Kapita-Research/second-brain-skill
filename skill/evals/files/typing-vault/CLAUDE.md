Standard obsidian-second-brain structure, with two optional modules on.

**`Outbox/`** — finished notes that belong to the firm. The only folder anything leaves by; the
destination is a shared drive. Nothing enters it except by the owner's explicit act.

**`Findings/`** — research figures (`type: finding`). A finding is quoted whole or not at all.

Filenames in English; other spellings live in `aliases`.
